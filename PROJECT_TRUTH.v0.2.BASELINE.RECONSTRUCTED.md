## Known baseline purpose

The v0.2 baseline existed to freeze the foundational Personal Librarian design before later milestone work modified the living Project Truth.

Its purpose was to preserve the original project assumptions, safety rules and architecture independently of future changes.

## Known foundational mission

Personal Librarian exists to consolidate scattered personal digital material into a trustworthy, locally controlled library.

Initial priority is photos and videos.

Known source families included:

- OneDrive
- Google Photos
- Current phone
- Dropbox
- Historical local drives
- Old phones
- Network / future server storage

## Known foundational workflow

The project follows:

`Discover -> Copy -> Verify -> Analyse -> Recommend -> Approve -> Act`

It must not follow:

`Discover -> Delete`

## Known baseline safety principles

- Acquisition is copy-first and non-destructive.
- Original sources remain untouched until replacements are verified.
- Synchronisation is not automatically equivalent to backup.
- Cloud placeholders are not equivalent to physically present local files.
- Sources remain separated during acquisition.
- Deduplication must not automatically destroy originals.
- Quarantine and rollback are preferred over immediate deletion.
- AI may assist classification and recommendations but must not autonomously perform destructive actions.
- Unusual and zero-byte files are preserved until analysed.

## Programme and library separation

Known foundational principle:

**The programme serves the library. The library does not belong to the programme.**

The application/repository and preserved personal data therefore remain logically separate.
